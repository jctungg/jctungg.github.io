Twimbit X

Our customers did not need more information.

They needed intelligence shaped around the account sitting in front of them. Twimbit X used AI to turn proprietary market data into account-relevant insight for customer-facing teams.

Start with the work the customer is trying to do. Not with AI.
02PRESENT

Curious East

What does an AI-native service actually look like?

Curious East helps people navigate the complexity of travel in China through an AI service built with a human in the loop approach. Agents support itinerary building and execution. People review the work, handle exceptions and stay accountable for the final experience.
